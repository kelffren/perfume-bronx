How to buy an authentic fragrance with confidence

Learn what to check about the fragrance version, packaging, price and delivery terms before buying an Arabian perfume.

Start with the exact name

A fragrance name may cover several versions. Ask for the full name, concentration and bottle size in milliliters. A photo of the actual bottle helps avoid confusion between variants.

Check the product, not just the box

Compare the label, sprayer, bottle base and packaging with manufacturer references. Batch codes can help identify a product, but a code or QR by itself does not prove authenticity.

Question absolute promises

Guaranteed 24-hour performance, unusually low prices or claims that a fragrance is identical to a luxury product deserve follow-up questions. Wear varies by skin and environment; perceived similarity is subjective.

Ask before paying

Confirm the final price, bottle size, availability, delivery fee, timeframe and how issues are handled. Request the details in writing. Avoid posting payment details or full addresses in public comments.

Try before deciding

A paper strip can help, but how a fragrance develops on skin can differ. If local in-person sampling is available, confirm conditions, availability and timing first; it is not guaranteed for every order.
