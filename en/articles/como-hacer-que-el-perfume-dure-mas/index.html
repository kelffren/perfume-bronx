How to make perfume last longer: a practical guide

Learn how to apply and store fragrance without exaggerated longevity promises.

Start with clean skin

Apply fragrance to clean skin. If your skin is dry, an unscented moisturizer can provide a comfortable base.

Do not rub your wrists

Let the fragrance dry naturally. Rubbing is unnecessary and may change your perception of opening notes.

Choose application points carefully

Start with a small amount and adjust for fragrance strength and shared spaces. Avoid eyes, mucous membranes and irritated skin.

Store it away from heat

Keep the bottle closed and out of direct sunlight or excessive heat. Do not leave it in a hot car.

How many hours will it last?

There is no universal number: composition, skin, climate and application affect the experience. We have not conducted controlled longevity tests.
