How to tell if a perfume is authentic

No single sign proves that every perfume is genuine. Consider the seller, the exact product variant, packaging details and official brand checks together.

Seller and provenance

Ask for a receipt, the exact variant, photos of the actual bottle and the seller’s details. An unusually low price warrants questions but does not by itself prove a counterfeit.

Packaging and batch codes

Compare the same size and variant against manufacturer references. Batch codes and barcodes can be copied; neither is an authenticity certificate.

Scent and performance

Storage and individual perception affect scent. Weak longevity alone cannot prove that a perfume is fake.

What to do if you suspect a fake

Keep receipts and photos, stop using a product if safety is in doubt, and contact the seller or manufacturer for a review.
