Is it for you?

Explore Asad if you want spice and an amber base. Say whether you plan to wear it in shared spaces or on outings; context matters alongside notes.

If you do not enjoy spice or tobacco in a scent description, compare Yara and Khamrah. A comparison with another perfume does not guarantee you will like this one.

Before ordering in the Bronx

Share the full fragrance name, quantity and ZIP code. Confirm the final total and delivery window. Delivery for one bottle has a fee to confirm; free delivery starts at two bottles within confirmed service areas.

How long does it last?

There is no guaranteed number of hours here. Skin, application and conditions affect the experience. We have not conducted a controlled wear test; choose by preferences rather than an unverified duration claim.
