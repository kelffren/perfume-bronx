Fragrance note dictionary

Oud
Resinous wood with scent profiles ranging from dry to deep. Not every Arabian fragrance contains oud.
Amber
A warm accord that can evoke resins, vanilla or balsamic notes; not necessarily one ingredient.
Musk
A scent family often described as clean, soft or enveloping.
Gourmand
Notes reminiscent of food and desserts, such as vanilla, praline and caramel.
Top notes
The first impression of a scent, which may change as it develops.
Base notes
Accords that are often perceived later as a fragrance develops.

Frequently asked questions

Does a QR code prove authenticity?

No. QR codes can be copied. Consider provenance, packaging and seller; no single detail guarantees authenticity.

How long does Arabian perfume last?

There is no universal number. Formula, skin, application and environment all matter.

Can I try before buying?

Ask whether in-person sampling is available in your area and confirm the conditions before ordering.
