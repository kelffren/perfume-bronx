02 / 06 — Contrast

Ask about scent families

Sweet, floral, spicy or woody are starting points, not guarantees of preference.

THE ASSUMPTION

The most popular will be the perfect gift.

Reconsider the idea ↗

Popularity does not reveal the recipient’s taste. A fragrance they already enjoy gives a more useful clue.

03 / 06 — Concepts

05 / 06 — Decision

Avoid assumptions

Do not infer preferences from age or gender; ask directly when possible.

You do not know their tastes. What would you ask first?

Which one sells the most?↗

It may offer sales context, but it does not describe this person’s tastes.

What fragrance do they wear, and what do they like about it?↗

Correct. That starting point helps compare profiles and avoids choosing only by fame or packaging.
