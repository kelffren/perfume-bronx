02 / 06 — Contrast

The formula matters

Concentration, composition and volatility matter, but an EDP label does not guarantee a set number of hours.

THE ASSUMPTION

If I no longer notice it, it is gone.

Reconsider the idea ↗

That observation alone does not measure it. Compare under similar conditions rather than relying on one impression.

05 / 06 — Decision

Olfactory adaptation

Olfactory adaptation may make a scent less noticeable to you even when others still detect it.

Which comparison helps more when evaluating two fragrances?

One on paper and another on skin, on different days.↗

Several conditions change at once. It becomes hard to attribute the difference to the fragrance.

Record surface, application and surroundings to compare similar conditions.↗

Correct. It is not a laboratory trial, but a consistent comparison helps more than an hours claim without context.

06 / 06 — Apply

Choose without false guarantees

Try it on your skin when possible and avoid treating advertised hours as guarantees.
