01 / 06 — Idea

What is a fragrance pyramid?

A way to describe how a fragrance may be perceived and evolve. It is not a chemical formula.

02 / 06 — Contrast

Top notes

The first impressions of a fragrance. Their presence and evolution vary by formula and person.

THE ASSUMPTION

The pyramid is a recipe.

Reconsider the idea ↗

It is a descriptive map: a note does not by itself identify a chemical ingredient.

03 / 06 — Concepts

Heart notes

The aromatic character that emerges as the initial impression changes.

01Top+

The opening impression of the scent.

02Heart+

The character emerging afterwards.

03Base+

Facets associated with later stages.

04 / 06 — Practice

Base notes

Accords associated with later stages. They do not guarantee a specific wear time.
