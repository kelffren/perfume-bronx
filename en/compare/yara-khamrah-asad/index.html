Three questions to narrow the choice

  1. Do you prefer floral sweetness, spiced sweetness or peppery amber? Start with Yara, Khamrah or Asad respectively and compare the full notes.
  2. Where will you wear it? Explain the setting and whether you prefer a discreet or noticeable presence. Notes alone do not establish projection.
  3. Is it for you or a gift? Ask what the recipient already wears before choosing an unfamiliar fragrance.

What this guide does not claim

This is not a wear test, sales ranking or guarantee of similarity to a designer fragrance. A shared note does not imply an identical formula. We do not assign invented longevity scores or customer ratings.

Buying two fragrances

Choose two scents you actually want rather than adding a bottle solely for delivery. Ask the store to confirm bundle eligibility, current price and your ZIP code before agreeing to an order.
