How to verify Lattafa perfume using its QR code

Lattafa documents a holographic security label and a verification process. Scan with your phone camera and check that the domain is exactly verify.lattafa.com.

Find the security label

Look for the holographic label on the packaging and inspect its visible details. Packaging may change, so refer to the manufacturer’s latest instructions.

Scan and inspect the domain

Use your phone camera. Before entering any details, verify that the address is verify.lattafa.com; avoid lookalike domains or unexpected redirects.

Interpret the result

Follow Lattafa’s published explanations. Invalid codes or unusually high scan counts deserve investigation. A photo of a QR code alone does not certify the bottle’s contents.

If the result is suspicious

Keep the box, label, receipt and photos. Contact Lattafa or the seller. Do not share sensitive information on unofficial websites.
