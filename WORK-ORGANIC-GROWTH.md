# WORK — ORGANIC GROWTH AUTOPILOT FOR OUD BRONX

## Mission
Continuously improve this real GitHub Pages storefront to earn qualified free search, image, referral and community traffic that results in verified WhatsApp conversations and paid orders. Do not stop at a plan: implement, test, commit and report each safe improvement during every Work session. This instruction is persistent guidance for future Work sessions, not authorization to run in the background.

## Read first
Read README.md, FUNDAMENTOS.md, SEO-LAUNCH.md and docs/kelodiamonds-mobile-audit/README.md. Inspect the entire repository and deployed site. Preserve the existing cinematic design, mobile carousel, videos, WhatsApp flow, branding and conversion UX. Never replace the app with a separate demo or redesign without approval. Inspect current main before editing; prefer a reviewable branch/PR for substantial changes.

## Recurring execution loop
1. Audit deployed canonical URLs, HTTP responses, indexability, titles, descriptions, canonical tags, robots, sitemap, broken links, HTML headings, mobile performance, Core Web Vitals, image sizes and alt text, internal links, accessibility and structured data. Report verified evidence versus assumptions.
2. Select the highest-value small batch of improvements by impact, effort, risk and verified demand. Implement the batch, validate syntax and mobile functionality, and commit with a descriptive message.
3. Update sitemap only with live canonical indexable URLs; create clean crawlable static HTML pages and direct HTML links. Avoid thin, duplicated or doorway location pages.
4. Build verified product pages for Yara, Khamrah and Asad first, then collections by real customer needs and stock. Include accurate photos, original descriptions, notes, sizes, prices, stock and delivery policies only if confirmed. Product/Offer schema must match visible content; test with Google Rich Results Test.
5. Publish a small batch of genuinely original bilingual EN/ES guides, comparisons and local shopping explainers grounded in verified facts and first-hand insights. Research query intent and competitor gaps before writing. No mass-generated low-value pages, fake reviews, invented wear tests or unsupported longevity claims.
6. Build a perfume discovery and comparison tool with unique, useful explanations, crawlable landing content and product links; do not make it a keyword-only page generator.
7. Optimize product imagery for Google Images and Lens, descriptive filenames, image sitemap where warranted, responsive sizes, lazy loading below the fold and stable hero media.
8. Add authentic customer Q&A and review collection with consent, moderation and spam prevention. No invented ratings, fake customer testimonials, fake blogs or link schemes. Mark paid creator partnerships appropriately.
9. Evaluate Google Merchant Center free listings and Google Business Profile eligibility; do not claim eligibility for an online-only operation. Only connect accounts and verify ownership with user authorization.
10. Establish privacy-aware measurement for search impressions, organic sessions, landing-page conversion, WhatsApp initiation and separately confirmed paid orders. Never invent analytics IDs, business phone, conversion data or access tokens.
11. Research legitimate outreach: relevant local directories, perfume bloggers, community recommendations and creator collaborations. Draft proposals; do not publish unsolicited spam or impersonate third parties.
12. At end of each session, update an execution log with: completed files and commit IDs, QA performed, pages published, observed metrics, blockers requiring user input and prioritized next 3 tasks. On next invocation resume from that log, re-audit live state and keep improving.

## Guardrails
No fabricated address, inventory, delivery promises, testimonials, reviews, links, media licenses or traffic claims. Never break existing sales UX. Respect Google's spam policies and legal advertising disclosures. Do not add analytics without considering privacy and consent. Never deploy a speculative large-scale SEO page factory. Search Console verification, real WhatsApp number, merchant eligibility and external account permissions are human-dependent tasks; leave clear steps rather than pretending they are complete.
